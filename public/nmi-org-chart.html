Company Overview

NMI is a global embedded payments infrastructure company selling exclusively through channel partners—ISOs, ISVs, PayFacs, VARs, and SaaS providers. The platform enables partners to deliver seamless payment experiences under their own brand. NMI provides a processor-agnostic gateway with 200+ active processor connections, terminal management, merchant onboarding, underwriting, risk management, CRM (IRIS CRM), and embedded lending (NMI Business Capital).
